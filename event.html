(03) — FAQ
Can first-year students take part?
Yes. Every track has a rookie prize for teams where all members are in Level 1 or Level 2.
Do we need to bring our own robot?
Yes, but if you don’t have one, join the Embedded Bootcamp on 14 November — every team leaves with a working line-follower.
Is accommodation provided?
Teams travelling from outside Dinajpur can request a shared dormitory spot when registering, subject to availability.
