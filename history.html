A decade
of sparks.

From a handful of students sharing one oscilloscope to six wings and a maker space — here’s how the current started flowing.

2004
Department

EEE starts at HSTU

The Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering is established under the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering.

2013
Department

First B.Sc. batch

The four-year B.Sc. in EEE begins — and with it, the first students asking for more lab time than the timetable allows.

2014
Club

The club is founded

Twelve students, one borrowed oscilloscope and a whiteboard. The first meeting votes on a name, a logo and a rule: everyone builds something.

2016
Competition

First robotics team

A line-follower named “Ohm Sweet Ohm” takes third place at a national contest and starts the Robotics wing.

2018
Flagship

Volt Rush is born

Our own inter-university robotics sprint. Eleven teams in year one; thirty-six by 2025.

2021
Publishing

Pulse magazine launches

During lockdown, members start a digital magazine to keep the club connected. Issue 01 is written entirely over video calls.

2023
Impact

Solar micro-grid project

The Power & Energy wing designs and installs a 5 kW solar micro-grid for a primary school outside Dinajpur.

2025
Department

M.Sc. in EEE begins

The department launches its M.Sc. programme, and the Research & Career wing starts thesis circles for postgraduates.

2026
Today

Six wings, 640+ members

A dedicated maker space, six focused wings and the busiest event calendar in the club’s history. Join us for what’s next →
