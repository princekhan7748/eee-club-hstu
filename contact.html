Who can join the EEE Club?
Any enrolled student of the Department of EEE at HSTU, from Level 1 to Level 4 and postgraduates. Students from other departments can attend selected events as guests.
Is there a membership fee?
Yes — ৳300 per session. It covers lab consumables, the welcome kit and discounted event registrations.
Do I need prior experience?
None at all. Every wing runs a beginner track in the first month, and most members built their first circuit with us.
How can a company sponsor an event?
Choose “Sponsorship” in the form above. We’ll send our sponsorship deck with packages for Volt Rush, workshops and Pulse magazine.
How do I verify my certificate?
Enter the ID printed on your certificate on the Verify page, or scan its QR code.
