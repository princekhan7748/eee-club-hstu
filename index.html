(03) — Wings
Six wings.
One circuit.

Every member plugs into a wing — a focused team with its own mentors, lab hours and build season.

W—01 Robotics & AutomationLine-followers, robotic arms and competition bots built from scratch. W—02 Power & EnergySolar micro-grids, smart metering and the future of the national grid. W—03 Embedded & IoTFrom bare-metal C on ARM to connected sensor networks on the edge. W—04 VLSI & ElectronicsAnalog design, PCB layout and digital logic down to the transistor. W—05 Signal & CommsDSP, RF, antennas and software-defined radio experiments. W—06 Research & CareerPaper-writing clinics, alumni mentoring and industry placements.
