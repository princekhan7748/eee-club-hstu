The EEE Club of HSTU is the student platform of the Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering — a place to tinker, build and compete beyond the syllabus.

Mission01 / 03
Learn it.

Give every EEE student hands-on access to tools, mentors and real problems from their first semester.

Vision02 / 03
Build it.

A department where every graduate has shipped real hardware before they leave campus.

Values03 / 03
Share it.

Open knowledge, safe labs, curiosity over grades, and always helping the next batch up.
